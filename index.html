Rivet Math

Boundary: arithmetic only Rivet Math applies named shop rules of thumb to the numbers you type: grip plus one and a half diameters for the cut length, two diameters for the edge distance, three for the pitch, and diameter plus clearance for the hole. It applies the rules as written, so it has not seen your joint and does not know the material, the rivet alloy, the head style, the loads or the holes you actually drilled, and it gives no verdict that a riveted joint will hold. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: the cut length for a 6 mm grip, the rivets along a 300 mm seam, the edge distance a 4 mm rivet asks for, and the hole for 0.1 mm of clearance.
