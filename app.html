Rivet Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page applies named shop rules of thumb to the numbers you type: grip plus one and a half diameters for the cut length, two diameters for the edge distance, three for the pitch, and diameter plus clearance for the hole. The diameters, grips, seams, margins and clearances are yours to measure or read off your own drawing. It applies the rules as written, so it has not seen your joint and does not know the material, the rivet alloy, the head style, the loads or the holes you actually drilled, and it gives no verdict that a riveted joint will hold. Every number here is your own entry.

The cut length truth

How long to cut the rivet: the grip plus one and a half diameters.

The seam count truth

How many rivets fit along a seam, given a margin at each end and a pitch.

The edge truth

The minimum edge distance and pitch a diameter asks for.

The hole truth

The hole to drill: the diameter plus the clearance.
